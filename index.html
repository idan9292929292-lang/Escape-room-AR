🔐 AR Escape Room

Game-master setup. Photograph each target in the room, from roughly where players will stand. The app learns them and builds the tracking map on this phone.

Tips for reliable tracking
  • Shoot straight-on, in the room's real lighting, and fill the frame with the target.
  • Targets need texture and detail. Plain white walls or screens track badly. For the projector, project a detailed slide or photograph the projector body.
  • The Person target is a photo of the person's face and shoulders. Faces aren't flat, so it works best when they face the camera like they did in the photo. A printed photo they wear or hold is the most reliable option.
  • For mailbox #16, frame it so the number and the box around it are clearly visible.
  • The Face to project image is optional. If you skip it, the Person photo is used.
